# Privacy Policy for Idle Orbit 2: Satellite Merge

**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

Sycamore Hill Studios ("we") makes Idle Orbit 2: Satellite Merge ("the game"). This page explains what data the game handles and why.

## No accounts or servers of our own

We don't run an account system or a server for the game. Your progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The game receives a record that a purchase was made so it can deliver it; we never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your satellites are full or a new event starts). They are created on the device; no data is sent to us. They never arrive between 22:00 and 09:00, and you can turn each kind off in the game's Settings or all of them in your phone's settings.

## What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't use any analytics service of our own, and we don't sell personal data.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
